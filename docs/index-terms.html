Index term list

This list gives 489 terms for the index: people, organisations, places, laws and policies, events and concepts. Each term is followed by the parts of the book in which it appears: the Prologue, Chapters 1 to 19 (by number) and the Epilogue. The locations were found by searching the text of each chapter, excluding its endnotes, so they record mentions rather than page numbers; page references will be added when the book is typeset. Names are given as the text gives them, with people listed surname first. Where a place or body has more than one name, the variants are merged under one heading, with the current name first and the older one in brackets, as in Mumbai (Bombay); the entry covers mentions under either name. Pseudonyms given by the sources are marked as such. Abbreviations used in the text follow the full name in brackets.
