## Prologue: Bangalore, 2026
A fresher waiting for an onboarding date that keeps moving, against a record-revenue industry. Must be built from documented reporting (named, published cases), not invented.

## Part I: Before software (1930s-1977)
1. **The statistician's machines.** Mahalanobis, the Indian Statistical Institute, Bhabha, TIFR and TIFRAC; computation for planning.
2. **Kanpur, 1963.** The IBM 1620 at IIT Kanpur, the Kanpur Indo-American Programme, the first computer science teaching and degrees.
3. **Self-reliance and its limits.** Bhabha Committee, Department of Electronics, Electronics Commission, ECIL, import controls, bank-union resistance to computerisation.
4. **Tata's experiment.** TCS in 1968, Burroughs, early export schemes, Patni.

## Part II: Bodies and wires (1977-1991)
5. **The year IBM left.** FERA, the Janata government, CMC, Indian minicomputer makers (HCL, Wipro, DCM).
6. **Body shopping.** Programmers on client sites abroad: contracts, visas, pay, lives; Infosys founded 1981.
7. **The training economy.** NIIT, Aptech, capitation-fee engineering colleges, AICTE, JEE coaching.
8. **Rajiv's computers.** 1984 and 1986 policies, Texas Instruments Bangalore and the satellite link, C-DAC and PARAM, railway reservations, Nasscom.

## Part III: The export machine (1991-2008)
9. **1991.** Crisis, liberalisation, Software Technology Parks, tax holidays.
10. **Bangalore.** How a city was chosen and what the industry did to it; Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, NCR as rivals.
11. **Y2K and Nasdaq.** Remediation work, the Infosys listing, ESOP wealth, the global delivery model.
12. **The engineer factory.** Campus mass hiring, training campuses, the pyramid, the bench, CMM; who got hired and who did not; women in IT.
13. **H-1B.** Visas, the diaspora, Silicon Valley Indians, TiE, returnees.
14. **Backlash and scale.** US offshoring politics, BPO, captive centres, SEZs, attrition.

## Part IV: Beyond services? (2008-2022)
15. **Satyam and Aadhaar.** A fraud, and building systems for India itself.
16. **The product question.** Startups, SaaS, UPI and India Stack, Jio and cheap data, GCCs.
17. **The pandemic boom.** Record hiring, attrition, moonlighting, the first unions.

## Part V: The break (2023-2026)
18. **The machines learn to code.** AI coding agents, fresher freezes, outcome-based pricing, the 2025 cuts, US visa changes, AICTE's response, GCCs as the new engine.

## Epilogue: India in 2035
Plausible paths, grounded in evidence, stated as scenarios.

## Comparative sidebars (only where they change the India argument)
US (throughout); UK; Soviet Union; Japan, Korea and Taiwan (hardware vs software); Ireland (tax-led FDI); Israel (defence R&D to products); China (domestic platforms, graduate glut); Philippines (process outsourcing); Eastern Europe (nearshore competition). Placement to be decided in Phase 1d.
