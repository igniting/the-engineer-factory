# Notes: software_exports.csv

**Built:** 2026-10-12 by `scripts/build_exports.py`. Values transcribed from cited pages and RBI HTML tables.

## What is in it
- **1980-1997/98 (two early series).**
  - Heeks (S0085, Table 1, printed p. 2): calendar years 1980-87, fiscal years from 1988/89.
  - Bhatnagar via Rajaraman (S0001, Table 2, printed p. 48): FY1990-91 to FY1997-98.
  - They differ by up to 10% in a given year. For example, FY1995-96 is $668m (Heeks) and $734m (Bhatnagar). Both are kept.
- **Nasscom: four points only.**
  - 1998-99 software exports $2.65bn (S0109);
  - 2006-07 IT-BPO exports $31.4bn (S0107);
  - FY2024-25E technology-industry exports $224.4bn (S0140);
  - FY26 estimate $246bn (S0306).

  The scope widens across these points, so they are not a series.
- **RBI.**
  - Survey figures for 2009-10 and 2010-11 (S0106, read via summariser).
  - Balance-of-payments 'Software services' receipts for 2018-19 to 2025-26 (Handbook Table 124). The values were unchanged across three editions; 2025-26 is preliminary.

## Gaps
1. **FY1998-99 to FY2017-18: no annual series.**
   - Older RBI Handbook editions with the BoP software line exist as PDFs on rbidocs (CAPTCHA-blocked).
   - The RBI survey (S0106) is in RBI Bulletins (rbidocs).
   - Nasscom Strategic Reviews are not public in full.

   Options: (a) WebFetch older Handbook HTML views by trying PublicationsView ids for earlier editions; (b) the author supplies Nasscom or RBI tables; (c) use DoE/MeitY annual reports, which print Nasscom export figures.
2. **The 1970s.** Only qualitative statements ('insignificant' in 1978, S0001, p. 29) and company claims (TCS over $1m in 1976, S0039). Not entered.
3. **Pre-1980 and early 1980s.** Heeks's sources are Heeks 1996 (S0070) and Dataquest; the underlying DoE figures were not read.

## Use in drafting
- Do not draw a single line from 1980 to 2026.
- Present the early series (Heeks or Bhatnagar, stating which), then RBI BoP for recent years. Mention Nasscom's own totals only with their definition.
- The net-versus-gross point (net about 40% of gross in the late 1990s, S0085) belongs in Chapters 6 and 9.
