# Notes: software_exports.csv

**Built:** 2026-10-12 by `scripts/build_exports.py`. Values transcribed from cited pages and RBI HTML tables.

## What is in it
- **1980-1997/98 (two early series).**
  - Heeks (S0085, Table 1, printed p. 2): calendar years 1980-87, fiscal years from 1988/89.
  - Bhatnagar via Rajaraman (S0001, Table 2, printed p. 48): FY1990-91 to FY1997-98.
  - They differ by up to 10% in a given year. For example, FY1995-96 is $668m (Heeks) and $734m (Bhatnagar). Both are kept.
- **Nasscom: four points only.**
  - 1998-99 software exports $2.65bn (S0109);
  - 2006-07 IT-BPO exports $31.4bn (S0107);
  - FY2024-25E technology-industry exports $224.4bn (S0140);
  - FY26 estimate $246bn (S0306).

  The scope widens across these points, so they are not a series.
- **RBI.**
  - Survey figures for 2009-10 and 2010-11 (S0106, read via summariser). They are lower than the BoP figures for the same years ($38.7bn vs $49.7bn in 2009-10), because the survey covers a sample of companies (S0106: 'nearly 78 per cent' of exports in 2010-11).
  - Balance-of-payments 'Software services' receipts, **2000-01 to 2025-26, complete**. Each year comes from the latest Handbook edition read (2008, 2014, 2017, 2018, 2024 and 2026 editions, all as HTML views); the edition id is in source_id.
  - Revisions between editions are small: 2013-14 was 69,439 in the 2014 edition and 69,483 in the 2018 edition; 2016-17 was 73,651 (P) and then 74,350 (PR).
  - 2025-26 is preliminary.

## Gaps
1. **FY1998-99 and FY1999-2000.** Only Nasscom's 1998-99 figure ($2.65bn). The 2008 Handbook edition's BoP table has no software line before 2000-01. Closed for 2000-01 onwards (see above).
   - **How the gap was closed.** Older Handbook editions still exist as HTML views on rbi.org.in. They were found by probing PublicationsView ids; the PDFs and XLSX files on rbidocs remain blocked.
2. **The 1970s.** Only qualitative statements ('insignificant' in 1978, S0001, p. 29) and company claims (TCS over $1m in 1976, S0039). Not entered.
3. **Pre-1980 and early 1980s.** Heeks's sources are Heeks 1996 (S0070) and Dataquest; the underlying DoE figures were not read.

## Use in drafting
- Do not draw a single line from 1980 to 2026.
- Present the early series (Heeks or Bhatnagar, stating which) to 1997-98, then RBI BoP from 2000-01. Mention Nasscom's own totals only with their definition.
- On RBI BoP, software receipts went from $6.3bn (2000-01) to $203.7bn (2025-26, preliminary). In real terms (2025 dollars) receipts rose about 16 times between 2000-01 and 2024-25 ($11.8bn to $184.2bn). Real values flattened between 2014-15 and 2017-18 (about $99-101bn), then grew again after 2018-19.
- The net-versus-gross point (net about 40% of gross in the late 1990s, S0085) belongs in Chapters 6 and 9.
