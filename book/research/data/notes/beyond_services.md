# Notes: gcc, foreign_firm_india_headcount, startup_funding, upi, computers_installed

**Built:** 2026-10-12 by `scripts/build_beyond.py`.

## The files
- **gcc.csv.** Consultant and industry-body counts only:
  - Zinnov 2008 via *EE Times*: 594 MNC R&D centres, 146,760 staff (read via summariser).
  - Nasscom-Zinnov: about 1,700 GCCs, 1.9 million staff, $64.6bn in FY2024; 2,117 GCCs, 2.36 million staff, $98.4bn in FY2026.
  - 2030 projections and Karnataka's 2029 target are flagged rows.
  - No independent count exists.
- **foreign_firm_india_headcount.csv.** Sparse:
  - IBM India 23,000 (early 2005), 39,000 (early 2006), 53,000 (February 2007).
  - Accenture's planned 35,000 by August 2007.
  - EPAM India 4,349 (2021) and 10,072 (2024).
  - Cognizant India 2022-25.
  - The plan's 'IBM 2010: c. 85,000' is not in Rajaraman's text: dropped as unverified.
- **startup_funding.csv.** Tracxn 2022-24 (via *The Week*, read via summariser), plus the three conflicting 2021 estimates (dispute xx). Never splice vendors.
- **upi.csv.** RBI Handbook payment-system tables, FY2016-17 to FY2023-24, from three editions (2021, 2025, 2026) as HTML views.
  - Transactions rose from 1.8 crore (0.018 billion) in FY2016-17 to 131.1 billion in FY2023-24; value from Rs 6,961 crore to Rs 199.95 lakh crore.
  - FY2024-25 is not in the editions read, and NPCI is unreachable.
  - The monthly point of 20.01 billion (August 2025) comes from press.
- **computers_installed.csv.** Early counts:
  - Menon: 15 by the end of 1964.
  - Committee on Automation: 30 (1965), 88 (1968), 140 (August 1971).
  - Rajaraman: fewer than 1,000 in 1978, 80,000 in 1990, over 60 million PCs in 2010; about 80 IBM 1401s in 1970; ECIL sold 98 (1971-78).
  - IBM installations at its exit: over 150 vs 800 (dispute s).
