## Gaps
1. **No annual series.** Nasscom Strategic Reviews give annual headcount but are not public in full. MeitY annual reports print Nasscom figures, and are worth a search in Phase 5 if annual detail is needed.
2. **PLFS-based levels (S0154, S0211)** were not obtained. NCAER (S0211) gives shares only, in summaries.
3. **Definitions widen** from 'software professionals' (1990s) to IT-BPO (2000s) to 'technology industry' (2020s), and include GCCs after the 2024 restatement. Growth rates computed across these points overstate growth in like-for-like terms. Say so in the text.

## Use in drafting
The broad arc (56,000 in 1990, 200,000 in 1998, about 2 million in 2008, about 6 million in 2026) can be stated with each figure attributed to NASSCOM or Nasscom and its definition. The book should not compute a single growth rate across all four.
