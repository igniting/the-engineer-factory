# Notes: fresher_hiring.csv and firm_women_attrition.csv

**Built:** 2026-10-12 by `scripts/build_hiring.py`.

## fresher_hiring.csv
**Infosys funnel.** Applications, tested, interviewed, offers and accepted, for 16 fiscal years from FY1997-98 to FY2025-26. It comes from the company's own annual recruitment statistics (Item 6.D of each 20-F; the 1999 prospectus for FY1997-98).

**What 'offers' means.** From FY2004 the 20-Fs report 'offers of employment', which include lateral hires, so the figures are not fresher counts. The early years read like campus intake but say 'job offers' without distinguishing.

**Offers per 1,000 applications:**

| Fiscal year | Offers per 1,000 |
|---|---|
| FY1997-98 | 25 |
| FY2001-02 | 9 |
| FY2004-05 | 11 |
| FY2009-10 and FY2011-12 | 65-67 |
| FY2020-21 | 34 |
| FY2021-22 (boom) | 38 |
| FY2023-24 | 11 |
| FY2025-26 | 15 |

Applications reached 5.9 million in both FY2021-22 and FY2025-26.

**Press points.** These are kept in a separate 'measure' column (fresher offers, joiners, plans, hires) and must not be compared with the Infosys funnel:
- TCS: FY26 'reportedly' over 44,000 hires; FY27 25,000 offers; 14,000 and 10,000 joiners in Q1 and Q2 FY27.
- HCLTech: over 11,000 in FY26.
- Infosys: a plan of 20,000 for FY27; 11,900 and 50,000 'new graduates' as reported via HR Katha.

**Gaps:**
- Infosys FY1998-99 to FY1999-2000, FY2002-03, FY2005-06 to FY2006-07, FY2010-11, FY2012-13 to FY2018-19 (in the corresponding 20-Fs on EDGAR);
- Wipro, Cognizant and HCLTech funnels (not disclosed in their filings as read).

## firm_women_attrition.csv
**Infosys attrition** is available for FY2005-06 to FY2011-12, from the 20-Fs' own comparisons, mostly excluding Infosys BPO and subsidiaries. Later 20-Fs read did not state an annual rate in text. Women's share appears only as 'nearly 40%' in a quoted statement in the FY2024 20-F.

**Cognizant (2020-2025).**
- **Total attrition:** 20.6% in 2020, 30.8% in 2021, 31.7% in 2022.
- **Change of metric:** from the 10-K for 2023 the headline is 'Voluntary Attrition - Tech Services': 25.6% in 2022, 13.8% in 2023, 15.9% in 2024, 13.9% in 2025.
- **Women's share:** 36% (2020), 38% (2021-24), 39% (2025).

**TCS.** FY2024-25: attrition 13.3% and women 35.2% (fact sheet, S0160, medium). 30 September 2026: attrition 13.3% (S0310).

**Gaps:**
- Infosys attrition from FY2012-13 onwards (quarterly fact sheets, S0161, site blocked);
- Wipro attrition and women's share;
- women's share before 2020 for all firms (M18 relies on NASSCOM and NCAER figures in the dossiers).
