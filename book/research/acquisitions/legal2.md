## 3. Murder of Prathibha Srikanth Murthy (ch14)

**Status: found, from court records.** The trial was in 2010, the High Court ruled in 2016 and the Supreme Court in 2023, with a clarification in December 2023.
- Trial: **Fast Track (Sessions) Court-XI, Bengaluru, S.C. No. 523/2006**, judgment of 6 October 2010 (sentence 6/8 October 2010). The court convicted under IPC ss. 366, 376 and 302:
  - 10 years' RI and a Rs 10,000 fine (s. 366);
  - 10 years' RI and a Rs 20,000 fine (s. 376);
  - for s. 302, 'RI for the rest of his life and shall not be released from jail'.
  This is as recited by the Karnataka HC, para 3.
- **Karnataka HC, *Shiva Kumar @ Shiva @ Shivamurthy v. State of Karnataka*, Crl. A. No. 245/2011 c/w Crl. A. No. 464/2011 (State, for enhancement to death), 31 May 2016**, H. Billappa and K.N. Phaneendra JJ. (judgment by Billappa J.). Citations: (2016) 4 Kant LJ 552; 2016 Cri LJ (NOC) 272 (Kar). https://indiankanoon.org/doc/188460426/
  - Para 7: the deceased 'was working in Hewlett Packard (HP) BPO situated in the Electronics City'. She left on '12/13.12.2005, by 2.00 a.m., by the allotted company vehicle route No.405'.
  - Para 126: 'keeping in view the age of the accused, he is married and he has no criminal antecedents and he is in custody throughout, in our considered view, the sentence imposed by the Trial Court is proper ... Accordingly, it is confirmed.' Both appeals were 'hereby dismissed'.
- **Supreme Court, *Shiva Kumar @ Shiva @ Shivamurthy v. State of Karnataka*, Criminal Appeal No. 942 of 2023 (from SLP (Crl.) No. 3400 of 2017), 28 March 2023**, Abhay S. Oka and Rajesh Bindal JJ., REPORTABLE. https://indiankanoon.org/doc/194820243/
  - Para 2: 'On 21st April 2017, notice was issued by this Court only on sentence.'
  - Para 13 (the legal holding): constitutional courts may impose a fixed term above 14 years even where death is not in issue. The 'fixed punishment cannot be for a period less than 14 years in view of the mandate of Section 433A of Cr.P.C.'
  - Para 15: 'It is true that the Trial Court could not have directed that the appellant shall not be released till the rest of his life.' The judgment also remarks on the case's context: 'The issue is of safety and security of women working with such companies.'
  - Para 17: 'We direct that the appellant shall undergo imprisonment for life. We also direct that the appellant shall be released only after he completes thirty years of actual sentence. The appeal is partly allowed to the above extent.'
  - Para 5 (the appellant's submissions): he was 22 at the time of the offence and had served 'approximately seventeen years and two months'.
- **SC, M.A. No. 1752 of 2023 in Crl. A. No. 942 of 2023, order of 6 December 2023** (Oka and Bindal JJ.): the ss. 366 and 376 sentences 'shall run concurrently with the substantive sentence for the offence punishable under Section 302', with set-off. https://indiankanoon.org/doc/6780606/
- Press, two outlets for the HC ruling:
  - *Deccan Herald*, 'Life sentence to Pratibha rape, murder convict upheld', 1 June 2016 (https://www.deccanherald.com/content/549808/life-sentence-pratibha-rape-murder.html): 'The bench dismissed the appeals and confirmed the orders passed by the fast-track court in 2010.'
  - *The News Minute*, 'K'taka HC upholds life sentence for Pratibha rape and murder accused', 1 June 2016 (https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/k-taka-hc-upholds-life-sentence-pratibha-rape-and-murder-accused-44157).
  - Earlier reports: *Oneindia*, 8 October 2010 ('The court had convicted Shiva Kumar of raping and murdering Pratibha on Wednesday, Oct 6'); *Deccan Herald*/PTI, 28 March 2011 (the HC appeal 'admitted before a division bench comprising Justices Ajit Gunjal and C R Kumaraswamy').
- **Conflicts and cautions:**
  - Her age: the SC (para 14) gives 'at the age of 28 years', TNM gives 27, and DH 2011 gives 'Twenty-two-year-old', probably confusing her with the accused. Use the SC's 28.
  - Days to recovery of the body: DH 2011 says 'a day later' and DH 2016 says 'three days later'. The HC record was not checked on this. Avoid stating it.
  - The spelling of her name varies: Pratibha/Prathibha, Srikanthamurthy/Srikantamurthy.
  - **Legal flag for the publisher:** the courts found rape as well as murder. The Supreme Court's 2023 judgment does not name her. Indian law restricts publishing the identity of a victim of rape, so the publisher's counsel should review naming her. Ch14 currently names her and mentions only murder. This statement of the law is not sourced in this file and is a flag, not advice.
