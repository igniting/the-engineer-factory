## 6. Framing corrections owed at drafting

1. **Ch. 19 and M30: 'fresher targets cut' (dispute hhh).** TCS's 25,000 FY27 figure was offers made by April 2026, with more on demand. About 24,000 had joined by September 2026, and TCS reportedly hired over 44,000 freshers in FY26 (S0309, S0310, S0138). Draft the fresher story as uncertain and uneven, not as a cut. Keep Business Standard's 'usual 40,000' only as that paper's framing.
2. **Prologue: pseudonym decision (author).** If 'Gayatri' is used, name the pseudonym as the reporter's in the first paragraph. Otherwise use the 13 February 2025 ministry directive as the opening.
3. **Conflict of interest.** Infosys names Anthropic's Claude Code (S0297), and coverage of Anthropic's Claude Cowork appears in S0142. Disclose at first mention in Ch. 19, in the epilogue and in the AI disclosure note.
4. **Ch. 12 sidebar.** Drop *Why did Britain lose its computer workforce?*. The recommended alternative is *How many engineers does India really produce?*, using disputes (ii) and M08.
5. **Ch. 17 sidebar.** The China sidebar cannot be drafted on present sources. Reframe it as *Why did India's biggest platforms come from the state?* (Aadhaar, UPI, IndiaAI), which ties to Scenario 4 in the epilogue.
6. **Composites and personas: never quote.**
   - BT 'Operation Satyam' (S0282) is a composite.
   - NITI's 'Rekha' and 'Aman' (S0316) are illustrative.
   - The People Matters relay of a Reddit post (S0304) was rejected.
7. **Ch. 13 link.** The 2025 H-1B proclamation's 'A 2017 study' is very probably Bound, Khanna and Morales. Confirm against S0254 and cross-reference.
8. **Ch. 15 update.** Add the FY26 GCC figures (2,117 GCCs, 2.36 million employees, $98.4bn; S0318), labelled as industry figures.
9. **Ch. 18 rank-and-file.** Consider moving the Mysuru trainees (S0311) into Ch. 18 as well as the prologue, since their wait began in the 2022 bust.
10. **Post-cutoff material.** Mark every item dated after June 2026 (the second proclamation, the court rulings, TCS Q2 FY27, the Infosys FY2026 20-F) for re-checking in Phase 5.
