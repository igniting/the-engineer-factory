## The outline's condition and what was found

The outline (v1) says the waiting fresher must be:
- a named person in published reporting; or
- a documented case in a filed complaint, labelled as a party's account.

Otherwise the prologue opens on a documented public moment.

**Search result.** No fresher named by their real name, whose joining date kept moving, was found in 2022-26 reporting. The searches covered:
- *Business Today*, *Deccan Herald*, *The News Minute*/*Newslaundry*, *Outlook Business*, *Republic*, *HR Katha* and *Rest of World*;
- web searches for named Infosys, TCS and Wipro recruits.

Every waiting or terminated fresher found is anonymous or pseudonymous.

**Best documented case: 'Gayatri (name changed)'.** She appears in Balakrishna Ganeshan's report, published on *Newslaundry* on 12 February 2025 (S0311). The text says 'TNM spoke to four trainees', so it appears to be a *News Minute* report carried by Newslaundry under their partnership; confirm the original TNM URL. What the report says of her:
- She is 24, from Andhra Pradesh.
- She graduated in 2022 and cleared Infosys's system engineer interview that year.
- She was 'left hanging for nearly two years' and onboarded in October 2024 'only after numerous emails and a legal intervention'.
- Her unpaid online 'pre-training' ran from July 2024.
- She failed the assessment, was summoned by email on 6 February 2025, signed a 'mutual separation agreement' on 7 February, and spent a night in a hotel before going home.

She is one real person, reported by a named journalist under a pseudonym the publication chose. She is not a composite. **Recommendation:** open on her, and say in the first paragraph that 'Gayatri' is the name the reporter gave her. The author decides whether this satisfies the outline condition. If not, the fallback is the documented public moment of 14 February 2025, when the Union Labour Ministry directed Karnataka to act (S0133), or TCS's results day on 8 October 2026 (S0138, S0310).

## Key facts for the prologue

**The arithmetic of joining.**
- NITES says Infosys issued about 2,000 offer letters on 2-3 September 2024 for 2022 campus hires (S0311, as NITES's account).
- Joining dates are given as 7 October (S0311, trainees) and 21 October 2024 (S0313, one trainee). Possibly two batches.
- Trainees' claim: of 930 who joined on 7 October, 160 cleared the assessment at the first attempt and 140 at the second (S0311).
- February 2025 terminations: about 300 (Infosys) or about 700 (NITES).

**Infosys's position.** 'All freshers get three attempts to clear the assessment, failing which they will not be able to continue with the organisation, as is also mentioned in their contract. This process has been in existence for over two decades' (S0311, S0133).

**The state.**
- The Union Labour Ministry twice directed Karnataka to act: 3 September 2024 (S0295) and 13 February 2025 (S0133).
- Karnataka's Labour Department reportedly found no layoff, because the recruits were apprentices (S0314; unnamed source via Moneycontrol). This connects to Ch. 18's Standing Orders exemption.

**The industry in the same years:**
- Nasscom FY26: $315bn and 59.5 lakh employees (S0306).
- Infosys FY2026 offers: 87,286 (S0297).
- TCS FY27: 25,000 offers, 24,000 joined by September 2026 (S0309, S0138, S0310).

## Disputes found
- **(ggg)** Joining date of the October 2024 Infosys batch: 7 October (S0311) vs 21 October (S0313). Possibly two batches.
- **(hhh)** TCS fresher numbers. *Business Standard* frames 25,000 as the FY27 target against a usual 40,000 (S0139). BT reports 25,000 offers 'so far', with more to depend on demand, and 'reportedly' over 44,000 hired in FY26 (S0309). So '25,000' is a floor of offers made by April, not a cap. That weakens the 'fresher target cut' framing in Ch. 19 and M30.
- **(iii)** The April 2025 Infosys round: 370 (Republic, S0313) vs 240 (BT/Moneycontrol, S0314).
- **(jjj)** The deadline to leave campus on 7 February 2025: 5 pm (S0311) vs '6 pm' (DH, via other outlets; DH page not read).
- **(kkk)** TCS Q2 FY27 headcount date: 'June 30, 2026' (BS, S0138) vs the September 2026 quarter (BT, S0310). BT is right; Q2 ends on 30 September.
