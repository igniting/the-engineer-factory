**Proclamation 10973 (S0136), 90 FR 46027-46029:**
- Opening, p. 46027: '...it has been deliberately exploited to replace, rather than supplement, American workers with lower-paid, lower- skilled labor.'
- Figures cited by the White House:
  - IT share of H-1B approvals rose from 32% (FY2003) to over 65%;
  - a '36 percent discount for H-1B "entry- level" positions';
  - New York Fed unemployment for computer science graduates aged 22-27 of 6.1%, and 7.5% for computer engineering;
  - 'A 2017 study' giving wages 2.6-5.1% higher and employment 6.1-10.8% higher absent foreign workers.

  The 2017 study is very probably Bound, Khanna and Morales; cross-check with S0254 in Ch. 13.
- Sec. 1(a): a payment of $100,000, with an expiry 12 months after the effective date of 12:01 a.m. EDT, 21 September 2025.
- Sec. 3(a): applies only to entries after the effective date.
- Sec. 4: wage-level rulemakings.

This is driven by where there is a skill mismatch, or, where we think that we have not been able to deploy someone. It is not because that we need less people'.
