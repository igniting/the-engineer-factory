In 2023 the Supreme Court, hearing his appeal against sentence, ordered that he serve at least thirty years before he could be released.[71] Its judgment treated the case as one about the industry: "The issue is of safety and security of women working with such companies."[72] UNITES won its first tranche of recruits after campaigning for better regulation of transport.[69]'
