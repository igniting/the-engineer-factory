# Phase 6 whole-book review (developmental read)

Read: all 21 bodies in order (scratchpad `p6/bodies.md`, c. 112,900 words incl. headings), `plan/outline.md`, `plan/style.md`, `plan/instructions.md`, `research/dossiers/phase2-review.md` section 6, drafting notes where needed, dossier section 7 (scenes) for every chapter. No chapter was edited. Item format: **chapter, passage ('identifying phrase'), problem, fix.**

Headline findings:
- The book's main defect is **repetition**. About 50 facts, quotes or scenes are told two to four times. Cutting them to cross-references will remove roughly 6,000-7,000 words.
- That cut takes the total from c. 112,900 to about 106,000, **below the brief's 110,000 floor**. Revision has to add words as well as cut them (section 7).
- About a dozen **forward promises are never kept**, among them TMA Pai, the Simputer, the 1994 World Bank warning, Blinder's test, the railway counters, and the histories of Pune, Chennai and the Delhi region.
- No chapter or the epilogue gives a **verdict on the working thesis**.
- Of the planned sidebars, only Ch. 12 and Ch. 15 have one, plus Ch. 17's reframed one. The rest were omitted, correctly, because their sources were unread.
